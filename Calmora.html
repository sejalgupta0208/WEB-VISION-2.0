Why Calmora

A different kind of companion

Wysa is a respected AI chatbot for wellbeing. Calmora takes another path: your words first, transparent reflections, and support that is local to India.

  • Core experience

    Wysa approach

    Chatbot-first conversations with an AI companion, plus CBT-style exercises.

    Calmora

    Diary-first. You express in your own words and own the story; tools come in around you.

  • Insights

    Wysa approach

    Mood tracking and conversation-based suggestions.

    Calmora

    Weekly reflections that cite the exact entries behind them, and can be dismissed if they feel wrong.

  • Language

    Wysa approach

    Depends on the product version and region.

    Calmora

    Built India-first: English, Hindi and natural Hinglish, with familiar Indian prompts.

  • Getting real help

    Wysa approach

    Self-help library and optional human coaching in some plans.

    Calmora

    A need-based finder for verified Indian helplines like Tele-MANAS, iCall and Vandrevala, with sources.

  • Privacy

    Wysa approach

    Anonymous use with policies that vary by product and partner.

    Calmora

    Entries stay on your device by default. One tap to export or delete everything. Reflections can be switched off.

  • Families and minors

    Wysa approach

    Offered through institutional and partner programmes.

    Calmora

    Consent-based guardian view that shares only mood trends, never the diary text.

Comparison based on publicly described features; offerings change over time. Calmora is a self-reflection tool and does not replace professional care.
